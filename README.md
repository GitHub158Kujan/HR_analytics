# HR Analytics Dashboard

## Overview
This dashboard analyzes employee attrition based on education, age, gender, job role, salary, and tenure.

## Key Metrics
- **Employees:** 1,470 | **Attrition:** 237 (16.1%)
- **Avg Salary:** 6.5K | **Avg Age:** 37 | **Avg Tenure:** 7 years

## Insights
- **Education:** Highest attrition in Life Sciences (41%) and Medical (32%).
- **Age:** 26-35 group has the highest attrition (606 employees).
- **Gender:** More males (858) left than females (577).
- **Salary:** Most attrition in <5K salary group (749 employees).
- **Tenure:** Peak attrition in first 2 years, then declines.
- **Job Role:** Sales Executives (328) and Research Scientists (292) have the highest turnover.

This dashboard helps HR teams address attrition effectively.
